## Review Notes (for App Store reviewer)

- The app requires a registered account to access health features. Use the test account credentials provided in the "Notes for Reviewer" field during submission.
- The AI chat assistant includes a medical disclaimer on first launch and on every session. It explicitly declines to diagnose conditions or recommend medication changes.
- HealthKit integration: the app requests HealthKit read permission for medications and lab results only. Permission request is shown in context with a clear explanation.
- Push notifications: medication reminder and health alert notifications do not contain PHI (names, diagnoses, specific medication details are redacted from notification payloads per HIPAA guidance).
- Camera access: used solely for document scanning (OCR extraction). Photos are processed locally and sent to the OCR pipeline; they are not stored as photos or uploaded to a photo library.
- The emergency info screen is accessible without authentication when the device is unlocked, by design — this is intentional for first-responder access.
