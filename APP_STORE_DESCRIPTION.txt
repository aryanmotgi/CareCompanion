CareCompanion — AI Care OS for Cancer Caregivers

When someone you love is fighting cancer, you become their full-time healthcare manager overnight. You're tracking a dozen medications, juggling oncologist appointments, decoding lab reports, fighting insurance denials, and doing it all while managing your own fear and exhaustion. CareCompanion was built for you.

CareCompanion is an AI-powered Care OS — a single command center that remembers everything, answers your hardest questions, and helps you navigate a healthcare system that was never designed with caregivers in mind.
